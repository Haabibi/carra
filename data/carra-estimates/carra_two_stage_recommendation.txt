RECOMMENDED PRODUCTION FLOW

STAGE A - DOCUMENT PARSER
Goal: document facts only.
Input: estimate/invoice/repair-order image or PDF.
Output: all fields except driver_view, plus confidence and source_text.
Temperature: low (0-0.2).

STAGE B - DRIVER EXPLAINER
Goal: transform verified Stage A facts into plain-language explanation and questions.
Input: Stage A JSON only (not raw OCR unless needed for evidence).
Output: driver_view + guardrails.
Temperature: low-medium (0.2-0.4).

Why two stages:
- Easier debugging.
- Lower hallucination risk.
- Lets Carra show which statements came from the shop document.
- Allows OCR/extraction model and explanation model to be evaluated separately.

FAIL-SAFE
If overall_confidence < 0.70, or total/line-item arithmetic does not reconcile, set needs_review=true and avoid strong explanatory claims about the uncertain fields.

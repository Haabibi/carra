IMPORTANT
The 24 PDFs in this package are ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENTS generated for Carra.
They do not reproduce a third-party template and contain no real customer data.
The public sources below were used to understand common field structures, terminology, authorization patterns, and document variety.

1. California Bureau of Automotive Repair - Write It Right
   https://www.bar.ca.gov/wir
   Use: official California reference for estimates, invoices, authorization, parts/labor documentation, third-party estimates, transmission estimates.

2. California BAR - Write It Right PDF
   https://www.bar.ca.gov/pdf/WriteItRight.pdf
   Use: official sample-document and compliance reference.

3. AutoLeap - Auto Repair Invoice Templates
   https://autoleap.com/tools/invoice-generator/
   Use: variation patterns including standard repair, parts/labor split, estimate-to-invoice, mechanic, tire, mobile mechanic and quick-lube invoices.

4. InvoSmith - Auto Repair Estimate Template
   https://invosmith.com/templates/auto-repair-estimate
   Use: standard estimate, diagnostic + recommended work, parts/labor split patterns.

5. EstimateWiz - Auto Repair Invoice
   https://estimatewiz.com/invoice-template/auto-repair
   Use: completed example with brake parts/labor, oil service, shop supplies and vehicle/mileage context.

6. FreeInvoicePDF - Auto Mechanic Invoice Example
   https://freeinvoicepdf.org/invoice-examples/mechanic/
   Use: completed invoice pattern with diagnostic, labor, brakes, fluids, supplies and tax.

7. StiffProof - Auto Repair Invoice Template
   https://stiffproof.com/invoice-template/auto-repair
   Use: diagnostic/parts/labor/shop-supplies invoice structure.

8. BayDocket - Auto Repair Estimate Template
   https://baydocket.com/tools/estimate-template
   Use: description, parts, labor hours, rate, amount, authorization.

9. MekaDesk - Auto Repair Order Template
   https://mekadesk.com/en/tools/auto-repair-order-template/
   Use: repair-order structure, vehicle condition, estimate, authorization limit.

10. Garage360 - Auto Repair Estimate Template
    https://garage360.io/templates/repair-estimate-template
    Use: recommended repairs, labor hours, parts costs, projected totals and authorization.

11. Smartsheet - Auto Repair Estimate Template
    https://www.smartsheet.com/sites/default/files/2025-09/IC-Auto-Repair-Estimate-Template_PDF.pdf
    Use: broad vehicle/service checklist and estimate layout.

12. InvoiceGenerator - Auto Repair Work Order Template
    https://invoicegenerator.com/templates/work-order/repair/auto
    Use: work-order / internal repair-order field structure.

DATASET DESIGN PRINCIPLE
The synthetic set intentionally mixes:
- estimates, invoices and repair orders
- parts/labor split vs compact layouts
- diagnostic-only vs repair recommendations
- approved / declined / pending / performed status
- additional authorization
- deposits and balances
- mobile mechanic fees
- tire, brake, HVAC, cooling, electrical, transmission and maintenance examples
- one two-page document
- one degraded simulated phone-photo scan
- one compact abbreviation-heavy receipt

Recommended MVP evaluation:
Document -> Structured JSON -> Plain-language explanation -> Questions to Ask


EV ADD-ON
Samples 25-30 are original synthetic BEV documents designed to exercise:
- high-voltage battery diagnostics
- AC charging inlet/system
- battery thermal management
- electric drive unit
- 12V/DC-DC low-voltage system
- regenerative + friction braking

No sample is intended as OEM repair guidance.

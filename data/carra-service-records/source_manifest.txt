Carra Service Record Dataset - Design Notes

All 24 PDFs in this package are ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC documents created for hackathon development and evaluation.
They contain no real customer data and do not reproduce third-party documents.

Document patterns included:
- completed repair records
- maintenance records
- diagnostic-only records
- inspection reports
- warranty repairs
- courtesy follow-up
- declined/deferred work
- additional authorization followed by completion
- multi-page history
- degraded phone-photo scan
- abbreviation-heavy compact receipt

The dataset is designed around the key service-history distinctions:
Customer Concern -> Technician Findings -> Work Performed -> Recommendations / Warranty.


EV ADD-ON
Samples 25-30 are original synthetic BEV documents designed to exercise:
- high-voltage battery diagnostics
- AC charging inlet/system
- battery thermal management
- electric drive unit
- 12V/DC-DC low-voltage system
- regenerative + friction braking

No sample is intended as OEM repair guidance.

RECOMMENDED FLOW

STAGE A - SERVICE RECORD PARSER
Input: maintenance record / invoice / diagnostic report / repair record.
Output: document facts only:
- vehicle
- mileage
- customer concern
- technician findings
- work performed
- status
- parts/labor/totals
- recommendations
- warranty

STAGE B - VEHICLE HISTORY EXPLAINER
Input: verified Stage A JSON.
Output:
- short driver-friendly service summary
- what was found
- what was done
- what to watch next
- useful follow-up questions

CRITICAL DISTINCTION
Finding != Work performed.
Recommendation != Work performed.
Declined item != Work performed.

FAIL-SAFE
If extraction confidence < 0.70, show a review flag and avoid creating a definitive vehicle-history statement from the uncertain field.

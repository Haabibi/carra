# Carra character concept v1

Concept exploration generated with the built-in image generation tool. This sheet is a design proposal, not a production avatar sprite or an implemented UI change.

## Direction
Carra is a patient companion who helps drivers read estimates and understand car terminology. Rounded automotive-reader housing, windshield-shaped face, subtle side-mirror tabs, and a folded-paper corner connect cars and documents. Ivory and slate keep the character compatible with the current light prototype and the original dark PRD.

## Suggested use
- Ask panel: head-only avatar at the existing 42px header and 28px message sizes. Simplify the fold and omit the embossed C at small sizes; verify actual-size readability before implementation.
- Upload and onboarding: full-body welcome or explaining pose, approximately 120–160px.
- Reading state: static paper-holding pose alongside accurate progress text.
- Reports: reserve the character for the Ask panel so the first viewport prioritizes estimate facts.
- Keep a calm expression for missing information and failures. Do not use expressions or gestures to imply that a repair, price, or vehicle is approved or safe.
- No idle animation; respect the PRD's motion restrictions.
- Use empty alt text beside an existing Carra label; use "Carra" when the image is the only identity label.

## Product fit
The PRD makes source-backed explanations and mechanic questions central. The character's job is to make asking approachable. Source citations remain the basis for trust.
The TODO and current styles implement a light report despite the original PRD's dark-only direction. This proposal follows the current prototype palette without changing app styles.

## Generation prompt
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one polished mascot character design presentation sheet for "carra", a US consumer app that explains auto repair estimates in plain English, shows source evidence, and helps drivers ask their mechanic questions. Carra is a patient, approachable companion for adults feeling unsure about cars. Design an original memorable character inspired by a friendly compact handheld automotive reader, with subtle automobile design cues, not a humanoid sci-fi robot.
Character: a palm-sized softly rounded rectangular ivory housing, slightly wider head/body unified into one simple silhouette, inset dark slate face panel shaped subtly like a car windshield, two clear warm ivory oval eyes, tiny restrained friendly curved smile. Small rounded side tabs suggesting side mirrors, short simple arms and feet, soft rubber slate sole details. A small understated embossed C on lower housing. Personality conveyed through attentive head tilt and open-handed gesture. Mature, calm, charming, gender-neutral, not babyish. Distinctive asymmetrical small folded-paper-shaped corner detail on housing connects to reading estimates. No antenna, no mechanical joints, no wrench, no mechanic uniform.
Style: refined matte 3D industrial-design character illustration, soft ceramic-like housing and rubber trim, restrained soft shadows, clean smooth geometry, not glossy toy, not photorealistic. Palette ivory #F4F5F2, slate #18262F, muted gray #AEB DC6 (use muted cool gray), neutral background #F2F6F8. No cyan glow or colorful status symbols.
Composition: landscape high-end brand concept sheet, generous negative space and precise editorial alignment. Left 55 percent one large three-quarter standing hero mascot offering an open hand. Right upper area two clean circular head-only avatar examples, one on white and one on dark slate, each same identity and readable face. Right lower area three smaller consistent poses: attentive neutral listening; reading a simple estimate paper; explaining with an open hand toward a paper. Expressions remain gently neutral and supportive, never alarmed or celebrating a result. Paper has only abstract gray lines, no fictional prices or claims. Main title small lowercase "carra", subtitle "A little clarity for your car." Small labels "Meet Carra", "Chat avatar", "Listening", "Reading", "Explaining" in clean sans serif sentence case. All text English and impeccably spelled. No other text.
Constraints: This is a mascot exploration sheet, not an app screenshot. Keep same mascot proportions and identity in all views. No sparkles, emoji, purple gradients, neon, holograms, shields, checkmarks, thumbs-up, diagnostic approval symbolism, giant eyes, big grin, or existing franchise resemblance. Professional and friendly enough to accompany a serious financial document.
